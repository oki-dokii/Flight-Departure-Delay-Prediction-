# Limitations and review dependencies

- Reporting-carrier domestic coverage limits transfer to international flights and other operators.
- Excluding cancellations and unusable departure records conditions the target population and undercounts scheduled congestion.
- January–October of one year cannot establish recurring seasonality or describe the unseen winter test regime.
- Tail assignment at the checkpoint is unverified; conditional features remain blocked pending P3 review.
- Outcome-assisted DST reconstruction and arrival-clock consistency need P1 review before inbound feature use.
- Historical monthly reporting does not prove real-time availability. P3 must state the operational-feed assumption.
- Carrier/airport rates reflect different route mixes and shared weather shocks; descriptive differences are not causal.
- Weather data, station mapping and licences remain pending P3 integration.
