# Limitations and review dependencies

- **Coverage and selection:** BTS reporting-carrier domestic nonstop coverage is not worldwide aviation. Cancellations and unusable departure records are excluded, so the target conditions on an operating flight with a reconstructible departure. Origin history counts omit those records and undercount scheduled demand. Carrier/airport effects are confounded by routes, schedule mix, operating conditions and shared shocks; descriptive associations are not causal.
- **Temporal coverage:** The Phase A development file has 5,749,041 flights and late fraction 0.2138314895; the common eligible target population is 5,749,040 after excluding the one flight already departed at the one-hour checkpoint. Development ends before 2025-10-31 UTC. January–October cannot characterize the unseen winter/holiday regime or recurring seasonality from one year. Final test starts 2025-11-02 UTC; test count and scores remain pending. No November/December flight data were read.
- **A-SCHED and reconstruction:** Schedule timestamps are assumed correct and known at T. The computed ambiguous-development-wall-clock count is zero (p2_evidence.json), using pinned timezone rules and a conservative union over US origin zones. This does not validate final schedules as a live feed. In DST-observing origins, local 01:00–01:59 on 2025-11-02 may have two UTC readings. prepare.departure_times uses DepDelay and actual local clocks to resolve candidates; P1 must address outcome-assisted schedule choice before the final run. Matching DepDelay is partly by construction.
- **D1 and operational availability:** Tail assignment is unverified at either checkpoint. Tail features are excluded from primary G0–G2 and pending G3 approval. An ablation would assume schedule/assignment/prior-leg arrival from an operational feed by T; retrospective monthly BTS does not establish that availability. FEATURE_SPEC.md's six-hour published-tail claim conflicts with the audit quarantine. P1/P3 must approve the feed/latency/continuity evidence. A-HISTORY is an explicit idealized event-time feed assumption for existing congestion; publication latency remains unmeasured.
- **Arrival exceptions and release identity:** The committed arrival_checks.csv records 153 scheduled-arrival clock mismatches, 79 actual-arrival mismatches and 16,783 actual arrivals not comparable. P1's separate Phase B release masks mismatches. P2 used the unmasked Phase A file, 265,113,328 bytes, SHA-256 8166d5f05de4ace74dd75d69ee2867686f45237105599a927f9a519382a29e57, and made no arrival-predictor or masking claim. G3 needs the masked release plus continuity checks, not raw arrival substitutions.
- **Weather and new-feature coverage:** P3's G2/G3 matrices, observation/report timestamps, station mapping, availability lag, units, licence and download date are pending. Weather stations may not represent all airport conditions; incomplete coverage and as-of joining affect deployment. No weather/inbound EDA was fabricated; Task 5 remains pending.
- **Feature interpretation:** Calendar predictors currently encode UTC, while EDA describes origin-local hour/day. Numeric month creates extrapolation under forward chaining; a no-month ablation is requested. CRSElapsedTime and Distance have zero source nulls in this release, but numeric coercion plus zero filling remains a future-input risk. P3 owns fixes/ablations.
- **Scale and uncertainty:** Population prevalence/decomposition use full eligible development; feature PSI and calibration use only the regenerated paired 20,000-flight pilot with rehearsal defaults. Full-scale matrices and selected configurations are pending. Seeds 42, 73, 101 vary the month-stratified training draw. Pooled population standard deviation over fold×seed mostly reflects fold drift, not independent seed uncertainty; see drift_seed_spread.csv for the separate within-fold spread. Calendar fields drift by construction. PSI thresholds are conventions, and small pilot category counts affect stability.
- **Calibration and model selection:** Day-block bootstrap intervals preserve shared within-day shocks, condition on fitted models/fixed bins and do not remove all cross-day dependence. ECE depends on bins and sample support. Class weights and scale_pos_weight can distort probabilities relative to prevalence; tuned configs must be rechecked. Selection and reporting on the same validation months can favor selected models. No calibrator was fitted on validation/test, and optional inner-training recalibration was deferred.

All numerical evidence above was read or computed by the P2 evidence, EDA, drift or calibration commands and is recorded in reports/tables/. Curated limitations, audit prose and review comments are separate from generator outputs.
