# Data collection and cohort

The source is the BTS Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance table for 2025. Carriers submit monthly flight-operation reports electronically under the reporting framework described in the [2025 technical directive](https://www.bts.gov/explore-topics-and-geography/modes/aviation/number-39-technical-directive-reporting-time). The coverage concerns scheduled domestic nonstop passenger operations of reporting carriers; it is not a census of worldwide aviation. Reported local clock times distinguish gate movement from takeoff and landing.

The project predicts gate departures at least 15 minutes late. P1 removes cancellations, exact duplicates and unusable departure records, matches origin timezones, and reconstructs UTC departures using reported elapsed delay with a separate local-clock consistency check. This reconstruction is not an independent validation of the delay measurement. Diverted flights with usable departure data remain in the cohort; missing tails remain explicit. The development interval is January 1 through October 31 exclusive, in UTC. All late-year holdout statistics are deferred.

Source archive URLs, SHA-256 values and original UTC download dates are recorded in `data/download_manifest.json` (reference downloads November 21, 2026). Licence references and airport metadata provenance are in `DATA_SOURCES.md`; the BTS source is public US government data and OurAirports metadata is public domain under the cited terms. The release parquet is a derived handoff, whose local hash is recorded separately in `reports/tables/provenance.json`. A later release download must not replace the source archive download dates in the report.
